# Power Supply & Mounting

## Power supply

- **Recommended:** ≥ 100 W PSU
- **Minimum (compute only, no PoE):** 25 W
- Input: **48–72 V DC** via M12 power connector

:::caution
Lower-rated supplies may work, but at your own risk.
:::
